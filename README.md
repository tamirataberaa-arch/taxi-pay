# Taxi Pay

Taxi Pay speaks the sender's name and amount aloud when a Telebirr, CBE or CBE Birr payment SMS arrives.

## Download
**[Download taxi-pay.apk](https://github.com/tamirataberaa-arch/taxi-pay/releases/latest/download/taxi-pay.apk)**

## Install (English)
1. Tap the download link above on your Android phone and open the downloaded file.
2. Tap **Install**. If the phone asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser.
3. If Google Play Protect says the app is blocked, tap **OK**, then in Play Store go to Profile > Play Protect > Settings and turn off "Scan apps with Play Protect" for a few minutes, install again, then turn it back on.
4. Open **Taxi Pay**. Tap **1. Allow SMS permission**, then **2. Allow background running**.
5. If the SMS permission is greyed out: hold the Taxi Pay icon > App info > three dots > **Allow restricted settings**, then try again.
6. Tap **Test announcement**. You should hear a voice.

3. Play Protect ከከለከለ፦ Play Store > Profile > Play Protect > Settings ገብተው "Scan apps with Play Protect" ለጥቂት ደቂቃ ያጥፉ፣ እንደገና ይጫኑ፣ ከዚያ መልሰው ያብሩት።

## Notes
- Everything is processed on the phone. No SMS is uploaded.
- An SMS shows that a payment was sent to you. Confirm important payments in your Telebirr or bank app.
